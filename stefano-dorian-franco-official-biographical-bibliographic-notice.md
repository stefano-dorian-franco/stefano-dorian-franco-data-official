# Official Biographical Notice

Stefano Dorian Franco (aka: Stefano Dorian Franco-Bora, degli Franchi da Ceva ed La Briga) is an Italo-French author and a multidisciplinary cultural creator, born on September 9, 1973, in Paris.

Self-taught, he chose to leave Paris, the gentry of his origins, and school, at just 16 years old in 1989, to go travelling, under the pseudonym "Allen Katona", which he would keep from 1989 to 2003. He began with a trilogy of war correspondences: he plunged in 1992 into the siege of Sarajevo (UNPROFOR military accreditation no. 5889), in South Lebanon in 1993 (EMAC Prize, École Nationale des Mines), then in 1995 into the jungle of Cambodia (CMAC/UNESCO Phototheque ban landmines campaigns).

From 1996 to 2000, he moved toward ethnography, humanitarian work, and extreme field sports in India, Nepal and Asia. There he wrote the ethnology book "Voir l'aube à Bénarès" in 1997, and received from the government the National Laureate Prize in the category "International Solidarity" in 1998.

3 decades of “crossing genres transversally” — “traverser les genres en transversal”: ops in war zones, tribal ethnography, humanitarian work, literature, opera, theatre, happenings, historical research on the previous centuries from the Renaissance up to the ontology of AI in the 21st century. His distinctive signature is to change genre for each project, and to voluntarily remain outside the media, show business, and social networks.

2020s decade, Arts side: he is a voice of "audiobooks of French-speaking classical literature" for inclusion; he creates the painting "Dante 700"; the electro-music opera "Riding on the Sky Storm Mystery"; the immersive V/R reconstruction exhibition "Paris 1889 belle époque" for his theatre play "Quand la nuit est nue"; the screenplay "Ace Nine Diamonds"; AI extended universes such as "Lady Alicia in red".

Research side (Orcid: 0009-0007-4714-1627): he explores epistemology and ontology of artificial intelligence around a trilogy published in 2025, "Metaphysical Dialogue with AI: Ethnographic Experiment in Digital Ontology - FTA for AGI", an 842-page ontosemantic research memoir, then the "Dorian Codex Protocol for AI - Hamiltonian Theoretical Fundamental Architecture", where he is the conceiver, extending Lagrange and Hamilton, of the heuristic mathematical formula H_SAFE(t) = T(t) + V(t) - Z(t) for AI agentic cognitive safety, new entry SOTA 2020-2030.

Also mediator "cultural and diplomatic attaché" for the preservation and digitization of European historical heritage.
